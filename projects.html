Adolescent NCD Watch & Risk Profiler

An interactive web application designed to evaluate and profile lifestyle-based non-communicable disease (NCD) risks among sub-Saharan African youth.

How the Application Works

1. Lifestyle Indicators Input

Users complete a targeted digital screener mapping out standard lifestyle vectors: physical inactivity days, screen time hours, diet quality, and tobacco usage facts.

2. WHO STEPwise Comparative Analysis

The system processes input metrics against standard regional WHO datasets across sub-Saharan Africa, contrasting personal indicators with national country-level benchmarks.

3. Risk Profiling & Interactive Charts

Outputs personalized risk levels (High, Moderate, Low), maps protective lifestyle guidelines, and constructs live multi-country comparison charts via Chart.js.

Echoes by EPIX

A digital triage and support tool built to meet young Nigerians where they are, providing a safe, stigma-free environment to process thoughts and access compassionate care.

How the Application Works

1. Culturally-Attuned Daily Drops

Users set up a completely local, private profile (using browser storage) and answer reflective prompts designed specifically for their environment (Nigeria, West Africa, the UK, or globally).

2. Shared Skies & Embers Fireplace

Release reflections as anonymous glowing stars in a collective night constellation, or completely burn heavy thoughts away in an interactive fireplace with dispersing bi-color embers.

3. Sensitive Triage & Breath Calibrations

A non-judgmental keyword scanner detects distress, references a user's pre-saved personal Anchor, highlights regional emergency channels (e.g. SURPIN/Mentally Aware in Nigeria), and directs them to the calming Breath Room.
