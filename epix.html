The Academy

Where Research Skills Are Built

The EPIX Academy trains students and young health professionals with practical, hands on research skills. Fully online, accessible across Nigeria, and no prior research experience required.

Three certification levels take you from complete beginner to published researcher. You leave with real skills, completed work and a network that continues supporting you long after the programme ends.

The Research Lab

Where African Evidence Gets Made

The EPIX Research Lab designs and conducts community based health studies, produces peer reviewed publications and develops digital health tools built from what our research actually finds.

Our current focus is Digital Health, Adolescent Health and NCDs, areas where Nigeria's evidence gap is most significant and where innovation has the most concrete potential for real-world change.

"We identify the gap. We research it. We build the solution. We test it in the community it was built for."
